Monthly tip

Clean water at home

Use boiled, filtered, or safely stored water for drinking and food preparation. Keep water containers covered and use a clean cup or tap.

Monthly tip

Food handling on market days

Wash hands before packaging produce, keep cooked food separate from raw food, and clean surfaces after use.

Monthly tip

When to seek care

Encourage families to visit a health facility quickly for danger signs such as high fever, severe dehydration, or breathing difficulty.
